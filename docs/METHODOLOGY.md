# Phase 1 methodology and boundary

## Source and time definitions

The authoritative source is `data/raw/Case_Study_1_Install_Base_Reactivation.csv`, SHA-256 `6863ea0014f050355bf9e0c5166e2284edfe8521ea3d87a2a2fa168177a229bc`. The pipeline validates the exact column order and types all source fields as strings before deriving analytical columns. It asserts row identity and original values remain unchanged. No source rows are discarded.

The default `analysis_as_of_date` is **2026-10-09** (configurable with `--as-of`). The **calendar** two-year proxy uses a date boundary: last booking on or before **2024-10-09**. `years_inactive >= 2` is a separate **source-reported** rule. We publish their cross-tab and `eligibility_comparison_status` (`agree_eligible`, `agree_ineligible`, `disagree`, `unknown`). The retained `eligibility_disagrees=False` only means no **confirmed** disagreement; it also covers an unknown comparison. Approximate calendar years (`days / 365.2425`) is descriptive only. A difference of at least 0.5 approximate years is a transparent review threshold for value disagreement, not a correction to the source. In the exception register the calculated reference is `comparison_value`, while `cleaned_value` stays empty for inactivity comparison issues.

The brief says **two fiscal years**, but provides no fiscal-year boundary, no precise definition of the rolling fiscal window, and no authoritative reference date for `years_inactive`. No fiscal-year eligibility is calculated. Before operational use, obtain the fiscal-year start, whether the rule means two completed fiscal years or a rolling interval, and booking-date provenance; then add a third explicitly labeled rule and reconciliation.

The date source has an exact mathematical pattern: booking dates advance with numeric account IDs, and `contract_start_date` is 365 days earlier. Within-root date ranks are therefore reverse numeric-ID ranks; adjacent date gaps are account-ID differences. The executed [window SQL](../sql/window_analytics.sql) is a **synthetic-date/source-pattern diagnostic**, not independent behavioral evidence. Its account-level output stays local. `LAG` and `LEAD` return adjacent *accounts' single last-booking dates*, never successive bookings of one account. A non-monotonic ID/date fixture in [test_sql.py](../tests/test_sql.py) ensures the SQL implementation still orders by actual dates, even though this source happens to be deterministic.

The same calendar proxy yields **1,269**, **1,634**, **2,000**, and **2,000** eligible rows at analysis dates 2024-06-22, 2025-06-22, 2026-06-22, and 2026-10-09. [analysis_date_sensitivity.csv](../outputs/phase1/analysis_date_sensitivity.csv) is computed by reusing `calendar_2y_proxy`, not by changing the default date. The source-reported `years_inactive` may reflect an unknown earlier snapshot. Even at a known date, two fiscal years are not necessarily a rolling two-calendar-year window. The brief's install-base prevalence claim cannot be verified without the broader denominator.

## Quality and hierarchy

Exact source strings remain in the cleaned CSV. France labels normalize by case and whitespace through a closed mapping. Literal `nan` and `Missing` health labels become unknown, missing partner flags remain unresolved, and ticket code 999 becomes null in the valid ticket measure. Raw values and the exception register preserve the distinction between unknown, sentinel, and genuine zero.

Root resolution follows parent links through multiple levels and reports duplicate IDs, missing parents, self-reference, or cycles. The source happens to have depth one. The Python resolver is recursive and is subject to the interpreter's recursion-depth limit; it is **not** claimed to handle unbounded depth. Joins use `source_row_number` as a one-to-one key; grouped spend sums each account row once. The root sum is an **additive recorded-spend proxy**. Parent rows may already contain child spend, so the group total may economically double count. We do not claim consolidated revenue or count the grouped sum again on top of account totals. Region attribution uses each row's own region; mixed-region roots are flagged.

The [parent-child spend comparison](../outputs/phase1/parent_child_spend_comparison.csv) shows **291 of 298 referenced parents** with parent-row spend below the sum of their direct children's spend. With nonnegative amounts, that contradicts a simple interpretation in which each parent amount fully contains every direct child's recorded amount on the same basis. It does **not** establish the true accounting period, whether some children overlap a parent amount, or the correct consolidated value. No source spend is adjusted. The additive source-row total, possible consolidated economic revenue, evidence against simple full-child inclusion, and unknown conventions must remain distinct.

Parent partner conflicts mean known 0 and known 1 coexist in a root group. Unknown values are counted separately. Mixed region and health states are metadata review flags, not proof of an error; subsidiaries may legitimately differ.

`has_manual_review_exceptions` (and compatibility alias `needs_manual_review`) means at least one exception was classified for manual review. It is **quality triage, not outreach authorization**. An account can have unknown health, partner, tickets, or inactivity even if this flag is False; no exception also does not prove fiscal eligibility. A later routing-readiness interface must fail closed unless a separately confirmed fiscal-eligibility decision, resolved hierarchy, required ownership, and all selected feature inputs have explicit acceptable states. It must record why any account fails a gate. No GTM routing engine or readiness score is implemented here.

Proposed **future interface only**: one row per `account_id` with `hierarchy_status`, independently approved `fiscal_eligibility_status` (`confirmed_eligible`, `confirmed_ineligible`, `unknown`), `ownership_status` (`known_partner`, `known_direct`, `unresolved`), and validity states for each approved routing feature (for example health and tickets). The output would be `routing_readiness_status` (`ready` or `blocked`) plus explicit `blocking_reasons` and a rule version. Defaults are `unknown`/`unresolved`/`blocked`; `eligibility_comparison_status` from Phase 1 is evidence for review, **not** an approved fiscal-eligibility decision. The selected feature list, thresholds, overrides, and owner must be approved before this interface is implemented.

Before Phase 2 consumes this approved source baseline, run `python -m everpure_case.source_integrity --repo-root .`. This separate gate verifies the authoritative CSV's exact SHA-256, 2,000 rows, and $2,541,024,291 recorded-spend sum using Decimal arithmetic. It rejects any byte change, including a one-cent spend edit; the generic Phase 1 pipeline remains usable with deliberately altered test fixtures.

## SQL interpretation

DuckDB executes the source-supported SQL files. CTEs aggregate spend and source-pattern date gaps. `DENSE_RANK` orders root and regional recorded exposure and ranks accounts within root or region. `LAG` and `LEAD` calculate calendar-day gaps between chronologically adjacent **different accounts' single last-booking dates** within the same root. In this exact source, those gaps reduce to account-ID arithmetic. They do not measure repeat bookings, ticket velocity, or within-account revenue change. Unresolved hierarchies have null root ranks rather than a misleading common null-root partition.

`sql/longitudinal_decay_template.sql` is an unexecuted contract for future **real** account-period revenue. Required grain is one account × fiscal period, with a consistent revenue definition and verified parent consolidation. A single source spend value cannot identify revenue decay, CAGR, reactivation probability, or NPV. The separate [synthetic methodology demonstration](SYNTHETIC_REVENUE_DEMO.md) **does execute** two DuckDB queries against five invented accounts and four invented periods. It guards missing periods and zero baselines, aggregates complete root periods before calculating changes, and defines signed USD change per fiscal period. Those outputs are labeled synthetic and never join to the source-derived totals or rankings.

Root-group spend concentration is a **recorded-exposure** view, not economic attractiveness: root group size and additive spend correlate at **0.881** in this file. `account_count` and `mean_recorded_spend_per_account_usd` accompany the total; regional mean spend per account provides the same size context. No predictive opportunity score is calculated.
